# Glossary

Every term this book coins or uses with a specific meaning, alphabetized. Each entry points to the chapter that teaches it in full.

This page exists because the linguistic gap creates the perceptual gap — it's hard to handle what you can't name. These are the names. For the book's claims rather than its names, see [The Lessons](./lessons.md).

---

## A

**Appropriate Response** — Matching your reaction's severity to the verified harm severity. Over-response creates new harm; under-response enables continued harm. → [Appropriate Response](./concepts/appropriate-response.md)

## B

**Belief-Blindness** — The inability to see past a belief so normalized it doesn't register as a belief at all. Not stupidity — invisible conditioning. Intelligence makes you better at defending a blind belief, not seeing through it. → [Why Rescuers Are Dangerous](./concepts/why-rescuers-are-dangerous.md)

**Body Stories** — Trauma stored in the body as automatic meaning-making patterns below conscious awareness. Not memories you recall — patterns that fire before conscious thought. → [Body Stories](./concepts/body-stories.md)

**Bottom Vulnerability** — Physical exposure while receiving: the bottom's body is in the top's hands. Has a safeword and cultural protection. Counterpart of top vulnerability, which has neither — see **Top and Bottom Vulnerability** under T. → [All Power Is Mutual](./concepts/all-power-is-mutual.md#top-and-bottom-vulnerability)

## C

**Caged Desire Loop** — Suppressing desire concentrates it; when it finally emerges, it's overwhelming — confirming the fear that caused the suppression. The fix is smaller, more frequent expression. → [Invisible Patterns](./concepts/invisible-patterns.md)

**Choose Your Lens** — You can prime your RAS for connection or danger before entering a space. What you consume beforehand shapes what you see. → [Trauma & Filters](./concepts/trauma-and-filters.md)

**Compass Stories** — Collecting stories, clips, and quotes that touch your heart as compass points toward who you actually want to be. Used to counter sinsickness. → [I Made a Mistake](./concepts/i-made-a-mistake.md)

**Complementary Filters** — Filters that attract each other like magnets. A predator-scanner and an attack-expecter will find each other and co-create the conflict both feared. → [Invisible Patterns](./concepts/invisible-patterns.md)

**Conservation of Power** — Power can't be created or destroyed, only transformed. When someone "takes" one form of power from you, you gain another. Victimhood becomes impossible to sustain. → [All Power Is Mutual](./concepts/all-power-is-mutual.md)

**Contributing vs Determining Factors** — Contributing factors are external conditions; determining factors are internal choices. Traffic contributes to being late; leaving late determines it. → [Responsibility](./concepts/responsibility.md)

## D

**Dangerous Gap** — The period between when a threat is made and when it's acted on. This is when the facilitator must act. → [Handling Threats of Violence](./concepts/handling-threats-of-violence.md)

**Dice Principle** — You can't control the roll, but you can control the size of the die. Luck in the moment is real; shrinking the die over time is your responsibility. → [Responsibility](./concepts/responsibility.md)

**Drama Triangle** — Stephen Karpman's model: Victim, Persecutor, Rescuer. The healthy alternatives (David Emerald): Creator, Challenger, Coach. The engine underneath the entire book. → [Drama Triangle](./concepts/drama-triangle.md)

## E

**Emergency Exception** — When imminent danger requires immediate action before full process. Not a loophole — a narrow condition for when someone is about to be physically harmed. → [When Things Go Wrong](./concepts/when-things-go-wrong.md)

**Emotional Mirroring** — Matching someone's emotional state when calm doesn't reach them. A de-escalation technique of last resort. → [When Things Go Wrong](./concepts/when-things-go-wrong.md)

**Everything Has Everything In It** — Every person, situation, and event contains all qualities at once — beauty and ugliness, hope and despair. Which one you perceive isn't dictated by reality but by where your attention lands; that selection becomes belief, then feeling, then action. → [Trauma & Filters](./concepts/trauma-and-filters.md#everything-has-everything-in-it)

**Explanation Trap** — When explaining yourself gets read as making excuses or manipulation. Common when someone is in narrative lock. → [Before You Judge](./concepts/before-you-judge.md)

**Exposure Therapy for Fawning** — Practice saying no in safe containers until the nervous system learns a new pattern. The pattern breaks through experience, not insight. → [Healing Fawning](./concepts/healing-fawning.md)

## F

**Factually Selective** — Being accurate about the aspect of the facts you're focused on while blind to the aspects you're not. You can be completely factual and still be working from half the truth. → [Trauma & Filters](./concepts/trauma-and-filters.md#everything-has-everything-in-it)

**Fawning** — Trauma response where someone says yes when they mean no. Not politeness — a deep automatic override that can suppress even survival instinct. → [Fawning](./concepts/fawning.md)

**Fawning Trap** — They said yes, so you can't know they meant no. The fawner's lie traps the other person in a dynamic they never consented to. → [Before You Judge](./concepts/before-you-judge.md)

**Fear Creates What It Fears** — The central pattern: the scanner's vigilance creates false accusations, the fawner's compliance creates violations, the cage's suppression creates overwhelm. → [Invisible Patterns](./concepts/invisible-patterns.md)

**First Officer** — A trusted person who challenges the facilitator privately and supports them publicly. The leader's side of the arrangement: weight input by closeness to the data — and after a wrong call, count the record instead of the story and keep leading. → [Walking Your Talk](./concepts/walking-your-talk.md#your-first-officer)

**First Plausible Explanation Trap** — The brain latches onto the first plausible explanation and stops looking for alternatives. → [Notice, Feel, Story](./tools/notice-feel-story.md)

**Framework Substitution** — Signal of narrative lock: they stop talking about the specific event and start invoking a larger system. They're no longer talking to you — they're talking to a category. → [Invisible Patterns](./concepts/invisible-patterns.md)

**Friction Check** — Screens for interpretive compatibility before play: Story Style, Repair Style, Communication Clarity. → [Before Play](./concepts/before-play.md)

## G

**Gift of No** — When someone says no, they protect both people. A clear no means their yes can be trusted. Hearing no creates more safety than hearing yes. → [Healing Fawning](./concepts/healing-fawning.md#the-gift-of-no)

**Good Thing** — An experience where everyone feels good both during AND after. The standard for play. → [Before Play](./concepts/before-play.md)

**Gun Test** — Self-check: would you trust yourself with a gun right now? If not, should you be wielding intimacy at MEDIUM severity? The question you ask yourself; asking someone else to run it is a [state check](./tools/gun-test.md#the-state-check). → [Gun Test](./tools/gun-test.md)

## H

**Harmful Belief** — A conscious but mistaken belief that drives harmful action. Requires education, not punishment. Third type in the Mistake Matrix. → [Types of Mistakes](./concepts/types-of-mistakes.md)

**Harmless Is Not Peaceful** — A person who can't be dangerous isn't choosing peace. True restraint requires having the capacity for destruction AND the wisdom to hold it. → [Harmless Is Not Peaceful](./concepts/harmless-is-not-peaceful.md)

**Hiding the Wound** — Concealing vulnerability to avoid rejection, which guarantees the rejection it was designed to prevent. The wound surfaces as a surprise, landing harder than if shown from the start. → [Healing Fawning](./concepts/healing-fawning.md)

**Horror Movie Effect** — Pre-framing your RAS to scan for threats so you find them everywhere, even when they don't exist. Like watching a horror film before walking through your own house. → [Trauma & Filters](./concepts/trauma-and-filters.md)

**Hundred Percent Control** — Both people have full power to stop or create the outcome. Not zero-sum — both hold 100% simultaneously. → [100% Control](./tools/100-percent-control.md)

## I

**Implicit Memory** — A memory stored in your body that you don't experience as a memory. It shows up as an emotion — fear, dread, rage — that feels like a response to right now, but is coming from back then. → [Body Stories](./concepts/body-stories.md)

**Influence Firewall** — Tool for detecting whether someone's words install fear/urgency/guilt or observation/curiosity/verification. Consent for ideas, not just touch. → [The Influence Firewall](./tools/influence-firewall.md)

**Interpretive Compatibility** — Alignment on how each person handles ambiguity and conflict. Assessed via the Friction Check. → [Before Play](./concepts/before-play.md)

## J

**Judgment Double-Edge** — The standard you set for others will eventually apply to you. The people who judge hardest collapse hardest when they make mistakes. → [Invisible Patterns](./concepts/invisible-patterns.md)

## M

**Mercy Defense** — Framing disproportionate harm as restraint: "I could have done worse." A named failure mode of over- and under-response. → [Appropriate Response](./concepts/appropriate-response.md)

**Mistake Matrix** — 2×2 grid: Malicious (intentional + conscious), Harmful Belief (unintentional + conscious), Accident/Unconscious (unintentional + unconscious). Fawning sits outside the matrix. → [Types of Mistakes](./concepts/types-of-mistakes.md)

**Mob Judgment** — Group condemning without verification; acting on vibes instead of facts. → [Appropriate Response](./concepts/appropriate-response.md)

**Motive Attribution** — Signal of narrative lock: they tell you WHY you did what you did, skipping the question entirely. Your internal experience no longer matters. → [Invisible Patterns](./concepts/invisible-patterns.md)

## N

**Narrative Lock** — When someone stops processing new information and filters everything to confirm their existing story. Three signals: motive attribution, framework substitution, no *real* repair path (the door is closed, **or** painted on — a "way back" that requires you to ratify the false story). → [Invisible Patterns](./concepts/invisible-patterns.md#narrative-lock)

**Narrative Lock on Yourself** — The same mechanism aimed inward: a story about your own capability ("I can't"), worth, or moral standing (sinsickness) held with certainty that filters out all counter-evidence. The belief is not the lock — the lock is the certainty *around* the belief. Genuine helplessness has a timestamp; the lock claims every moment still to come. → [Invisible Patterns](./concepts/invisible-patterns.md#narrative-lock-on-yourself)

**Notice, Feel, Story** — Three-step framework separating observation (what a camera would record), emotion (what you feel), and interpretation (the story you made). → [Notice, Feel, Story](./tools/notice-feel-story.md)

## O

**Omniresponsible** — Your influence extends to everything you touch and everything you don't. A posture, not a burden. → [Responsibility](./concepts/responsibility.md)

**Order of Operations** — For threats: (1) get the attacked person to safety, (2) contain violent person, (3) remove violent person, (4) address crowd. → [Handling Threats of Violence](./concepts/handling-threats-of-violence.md)

**Over-Response** — Responding with higher severity than the harm warrants. Creates new harm. The most common inappropriate response. → [Appropriate Response](./concepts/appropriate-response.md)

## P

**Painted-On Door** — A repair path that appears to exist but only opens if you agree with the accuser's story — accept the assigned motive, confirm the category, take on the label. Not repair (owning *what you did*) but a confession (ratifying *who they've decided you are*). → [Invisible Patterns](./concepts/invisible-patterns.md)

**Popcorn Metaphor** — Unconscious mistakes are like burning popcorn: you weren't thinking, not choosing to harm. You don't punish someone for burned popcorn. → [Types of Mistakes](./concepts/types-of-mistakes.md)

**Pre-Framing** — What happens before you enter a situation shapes how you perceive it. The lens you put on before walking through the door. → [Trauma & Filters](./concepts/trauma-and-filters.md)

**Primary Sources** — The people directly involved. Verify with them before judging. → [Before You Judge](./concepts/before-you-judge.md)

**Promise, The** — Facilitator's explicit public commitment: these are the rules, these are the consequences, and good-faith mistakes will be treated as mistakes. → [Before You Facilitate](./concepts/before-you-facilitate.md#the-promise)

**Pseudo-Feelings** — Statements that sound like feelings but are claims about others. "I feel abandoned" = "you abandoned me." → [Notice, Feel, Story](./tools/notice-feel-story.md)

## Q

**Qualified Source** — Someone who has verified with primary sources before acting. Unqualified sources + disciplinary action = automatic over-response. → [Before You Judge](./concepts/before-you-judge.md)

## R

**RBDSMT** — Pre-play conversation framework: Relationships, Boundaries, Desires, Sexual Health, Meaning & Mistakes, Trauma. Based on ISTA practices. → [Before Play](./concepts/before-play.md)

**Real Repair vs Fake Resolution** — Real: both own their part and feel complete. Fake: one fawns or one takes zero responsibility. → [Guiding Public Repair](./concepts/guiding-public-repair.md)

**Reformed Righteous Predator** — A righteous predator who woke up, owns the harm they caused, and fights to be different. Has unique insight into the pattern from the inside. → [I Made a Mistake](./concepts/i-made-a-mistake.md)

**Replacing the Sentence** — Finding the body's underlying story, writing its precise inverse, and speaking it out loud in a peak emotional state daily until the body believes the new version. → [Invisible Patterns](./concepts/invisible-patterns.md)

**Responsibility Mirror** — Before pointing at others, look at your part. → [Responsibility](./concepts/responsibility.md)

**Responsibility Triad** — Responsibility (seeing your causal part) vs Accountability (making repair) vs Punishment (inflicting suffering). Most people conflate all three. → [Responsibility](./concepts/responsibility.md)

**Reverse Fawning** — Saying no when you mean yes. Suppressing desire because expressing it feels dangerous. The mirror image of fawning. → [Healing Fawning](./concepts/healing-fawning.md)

**Righteous Predator** — Someone who causes significant harm powered by moral certainty instead of selfishness. Has everything that makes harmful beliefs dangerous, plus urgency, moral certainty, and the ability to organize. → [Why Rescuers Are Dangerous](./concepts/why-rescuers-are-dangerous.md)

## S

**Safest Place in the World** — Play spaces with facilitators, agreements, and 30 people ready to intervene on your behalf. The worst place for a selfish predator to operate — yet many still fawn. → [Fawning](./concepts/fawning.md)

**Scorched Earth** — Treating accidents like malice; disproportionate response that destroys everything. → [Appropriate Response](./concepts/appropriate-response.md)

**Selfish Predator** — Someone who knowingly causes harm for their own benefit. Far rarer than righteous predators, and limited in scale because "let's be evil" doesn't recruit. → [Why Rescuers Are Dangerous](./concepts/why-rescuers-are-dangerous.md)

**Selfless Defense** — A play on self-defense: standing your ground not (only) to protect yourself, but to protect everyone the precedent would reach. The action is identical to self-defense; the source is opposite. → [All Power Is Mutual](./concepts/all-power-is-mutual.md)

**Severity Model** — LOW (inconvenience, recoverable quickly), MEDIUM (real impact, recoverable with effort), HIGH (permanent or near-permanent damage). → [Severity](./concepts/severity.md)

**Severity Shift** — In play spaces you cross into MEDIUM severity by default. Mistakes have real consequences. → [Severity](./concepts/severity.md)

**Sinsickness** — Moral immune system attacking you. Shame that attacks identity rather than behavior: "I AM bad" instead of "I DID something bad." Can be fatal to growth. → [I Made a Mistake](./concepts/i-made-a-mistake.md#sinsickness)

**State Check** — Asking someone else to run their Gun Test: walk up and say "state check." The only spoken form of the tool — the gun stays on the page, never in the air. Lineage: the question asks about your *state* and a *gun* → "gun state check" → speak the last half. → [The State Check](./tools/gun-test.md#the-state-check)

**Story Style** — Part of the Friction Check: how someone interprets ambiguity — with curiosity or blame. → [Before Play](./concepts/before-play.md)

## T

**Top and Bottom Vulnerability** — Bottom vulnerability (physical exposure) has a safeword and cultural protection. Top vulnerability (reputational exposure) has neither. The person who looks least vulnerable is usually most vulnerable. → [All Power Is Mutual](./concepts/all-power-is-mutual.md#top-and-bottom-vulnerability)

**Trust Baseline** — Bidirectional trust that a mistake will be treated as a mistake. If either person can't trust that, play is premature. → [Before Play](./concepts/before-play.md)

**Two Victims Problem** — When fawning happens, both the fawner (who had an unwanted experience) and the other person (who was lied to about consent) are harmed. → [Fawning](./concepts/fawning.md)

## U

**Under-Response** — Responding with lower severity than the harm warrants. Enables continued harm. Often driven by fear or fawning. → [Appropriate Response](./concepts/appropriate-response.md)

## V

**Victim Position Is Powerful** — Crying victim mobilizes rescuers, gains moral authority, can justify persecution. The Drama Triangle's Victim role isn't powerless — it's enormously powerful. → [All Power Is Mutual](./concepts/all-power-is-mutual.md)

**Vulnerability Flip** — After an incident, the physically helpless person often holds most power (moral authority, cultural sympathy, narrative control), while the person who had physical freedom faces the most catastrophic consequences. → [All Power Is Mutual](./concepts/all-power-is-mutual.md)

## W

**Witch Hunt Dynamic** — Accusation → fear activation → punishment without verification. The labels change (witch → heretic → communist → predator) but the pattern is identical. → [Before You Judge](./concepts/before-you-judge.md)
