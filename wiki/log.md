## [2026-07-05] add | "It's All Risky" section in `walking-your-talk.md` — the facilitator risk calculus (Jim Rohn)

**Book change — author-initiated, post-freeze addition (author explicitly overrode the fable-review-plan freeze; model assessed 8–9x):** new `### It's All Risky` section in `walking-your-talk.md`, between "serving, not pleasing" and "Protecting Top Vulnerability." Fills a real gap: the book told facilitators *what's right*, *how to hold it* (the Promise, First Officer), and *what happens to the community* if they don't (punishment-culture loop) — but never answered the objection that actually runs the decision under fear: *"the right call puts the risk on me; caving is safer; why volunteer?"* The section attacks the premise (there is no risk-free option) via Jim Rohn's "It's all risky. Argument: the bill for caving arrives on a delay (intimidation shown to work, promise visibly worthless, clearest eyes leave first, punishment culture compounds, clear-seeing observers accurately conclude the container is not run well) — and the anger you were avoiding gets *routed, not avoided* (narrative-locked anger for holding the line vs. quiet loss of trust from the clear-eyed for caving). Bolded book-voice distillations: "You don't get to choose whether people come after you. You only get to choose what they come after you for — and what it buys." and "You were never choosing between risk and safety. You were choosing which bill." `wiki/book-overview.md` Part 8 updated. Rohn joins the quoted-sources roster (flag for the production-checklist quotes audit distribution check).
