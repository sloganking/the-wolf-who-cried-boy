## Notes & open items

- **Endorsement slot (reserved).** A respected, well-known facilitator's genuine endorsement would slot under the hook or on the back cover, with full credentials. Add only once they've actually read it and chosen to endorse — never preemptively. (Specific names and outreach strategy live in local, untracked notes.)
- **An endorsement from someone personally involved in a past incident is not the right tool here.** It re-centers the incident, means little to the general buyer, and carries consent and optics costs. Keep any genuine goodwill private; don't build the page on it.
- **Author tag** points to coaching on purpose — the book functions as a credibility instrument for the 1-on-1 practice.
- **Values this copy is held to:** it's gold / every line earns its place · vulnerable without victimhood · no moralizing, no labels, don't repeat fiction · inarguable, show don't tell · feeling over explanation (be the wizard, show the crystal) · sight, not sides · bigger than the temple + pointed inward · the medium is the message · clarity / non-ambiguity.
